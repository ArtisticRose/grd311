Pumpkin Seeds

Roasted pumpkin seeds can be considered the seasonal snack of fall, delicious, with a savoury taste! You may have even grown up making them right after carving your Halloween pumpkin, separating the seeds from all of those orange slimy pumpkin innards. I'm here to tell you how to make this simple fall snack! It is the perfect tasty treat when you just need something small and can be shelf stable for the whole season so if you don’t get through it all, no worries, you have plenty of time to devour them!

Now while this recipe says to season your seeds with salt, it is really up to you, many other seasonings could be substituted and added in for your unique flavour preferences! This recipe should yield about 4 servings. Be sure to enjoy it!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup raw pumpkin seeds
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 300°F (150°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Rinse the pumpkin seeds in a colander under cold water to remove any pulp or strings. Pat them dry with a paper towel.
  3. In a bowl, toss the seeds with olive oil, and salt.
  4. Spread the seeds in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet.
  5. Bake for 20-30 minutes, flipping them around occasionally, until the seeds are golden brown and crispy.
  6. Remove from the oven and let them cool completely before serving.
  7. Enjoy your homemade snack!

Pumpkin Sugar Cookies

Every significant season growing up I would make sugar cookies with my grandmother in funny little shapes relevant to whatever holiday was closest! So christmas would be christmas trees, easter would be eggs, and halloween would be pumpkins! I’m here to tell you how to make my grandmother's pumpkin shaped sugar cookies for your hungering sweet tooth!

This recipe should only take about 30 minutes to make, translating to 10 minutes of prep time and 18 minutes of cooking time if all goes well in the kitchen! These measurements will leave you with 42 delicious cookies. All you will need are your basic cooking utensils, be sure to enjoy it, and have fun baking!

Ingredients

  • 2 sticks (16 tablespoons) butter
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 300°F (150°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. In a large bowl, cream the butter and both sugars until light and fluffy.
  3. Beat in the egg and vanilla extract.
  4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the baking soda and salt.
  5. Gradually add the flour and the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, mixing until just combined.
  6. Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto the prepared baking sheet.
  7. Bake for 10-12 minutes, or until the edges are golden brown.
  8. Remove from the oven and let them cool on the baking sheet for a few minutes before transferring to a wire rack.
  9. Enjoy your homemade treat!
